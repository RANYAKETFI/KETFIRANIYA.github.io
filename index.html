QA-TimeGAN: quality-aware data generation

A time-series GAN that uses a data-quality index as a training signal, with anatomical weighting and a symmetry constraint, to augment a small clinical dataset safely.

Sensitivity 0.74 → 0.89 · ROC-AUC 0.638 → 0.831

An 8-dimension, expert-weighted quality score at sensor and session level. Used to monitor acquisitions and to flag sessions a model is likely to misclassify.
